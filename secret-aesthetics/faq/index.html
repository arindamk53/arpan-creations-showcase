Treatments

Anti-wrinkle treatment (botulinum toxin / Botox) works by temporarily relaxing the muscles that cause dynamic wrinkles — such as frown lines, forehead lines and crow's feet. Results last 3–4 months. Dermal fillers use hyaluronic acid to physically add volume and reshape areas — such as lips, cheeks, or hollows. Results last 6–18 months. Both are injectable treatments performed by our doctors.
Endolift® is a revolutionary Italian laser technology that uses ultra-fine optical fibres to deliver laser energy beneath the skin — causing immediate tissue contraction and long-term collagen stimulation. It is Birmingham's most advanced non-surgical face and neck lifting procedure. Dr Naqvi obtained specialist certification from the Italian Endolift® Academy and is the only certified provider in Birmingham and the West Midlands.
Profhilo is a hyaluronic acid-based injectable treatment designed to deeply hydrate skin and stimulate collagen production — without adding volume or changing facial shape. It is not a filler. It spreads through the tissues, hydrating from within, and results in visibly improved skin quality, texture, and plumpness. A course of 2 sessions is recommended, spaced one month apart.
No. All non-surgical aesthetic treatments are temporary to varying degrees. Anti-wrinkle: 3–4 months. Dermal fillers: 6–18 months. Profhilo: 6–12 months. Endolift®: 3–5 years. Results vary between individuals and are influenced by age, lifestyle, and skin health. Your doctor will discuss expected longevity at your consultation.

Safety & Regulation

We use only premium, CE-marked or FDA-approved products from reputable manufacturers. For injectables, we predominantly use products from Galderma (Juvederm / Restylane range), Allergan, and Merz — manufacturers with robust safety records and extensive clinical evidence. We do not use unbranded, unlicensed, or unverified products.
As a CQC-regulated medical facility, we have clear protocols for managing any adverse events. Our doctors are trained in complication management including hyaluronidase administration for filler dissolving, and maintain appropriate medical supplies on-site. All patients receive 24/7 access to a clinical phone line following treatment. We carry comprehensive medical indemnity insurance. In the extremely rare event of a serious complication, we work directly with local NHS teams.
No. We do not perform any aesthetic treatments — including Botox, fillers, laser, or Profhilo — during pregnancy or while breastfeeding. This is a medical safeguard in line with national clinical guidelines. Please advise us if you are pregnant or breastfeeding and we will reschedule your appointment accordingly.

Aftercare

For 24 hours following anti-wrinkle treatment, please avoid: vigorous exercise, alcohol, hot baths/saunas, lying flat for 4 hours, and touching or rubbing the treated area. You may apply makeup after 4 hours. Full aftercare instructions are provided at your appointment.
Some swelling and mild bruising is normal following filler treatment — particularly lip filler. Swelling typically peaks at 24–48 hours and resolves within 3–5 days. Final results are visible at 2 weeks, at which point your complimentary review appointment is scheduled.
Most Endolift® patients experience mild redness and slight swelling for 24–48 hours. Micro-puncture marks (if visible at all) resolve within hours. Most patients return to work the same day or the following morning. No dressings or bandaging are required. This is considerably less downtime than any surgical alternative.
