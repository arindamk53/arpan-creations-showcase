ABOUT US

Where Expertise
Meets Elegance.

Founded by Dr Naqvi and Dr Zoya, Secret Aesthetics was born from a singular vision: to deliver world-class, doctor-led aesthetic medicine within a setting as refined as the results we create. Housed exclusively within Harvey Nichols at The Mailbox, Birmingham — every consultation, every treatment is performed personally by our founding physicians.

We are proud Galderma UK Faculty members, CQC-regulated, and Birmingham's sole providers of the Endolift® laser lifting procedure. Our approach is clinical, personalised and entirely non-surgical — delivering surgical-grade results without the associated risks or recovery time.

SIGNATURE TREATMENT

Endolift®
Laser Face & Neck Lifting

The most advanced non-surgical face and neck lifting technology in the world — and we're Birmingham's only certified providers. Endolift® uses a revolutionary micro-laser fibre inserted beneath the skin to contract tissue and stimulate collagen from within. The result? Dramatic, surgical-quality lifting with zero downtime.

✦ 60–90 minute procedure ✦ Zero downtime ✦ Results last 3–5 years ✦ Birmingham exclusive ✦ From £1,500

Halo + BBL Hero®
The Gold Standard in Skin Renewal

The Halo Hybrid Fractional Laser and BroadBand Light (BBL) Hero® represent the pinnacle of medical laser technology. Together, they address skin tone, texture, fine lines, sun damage and pigmentation — in a single, physician-administered session. Birmingham's first and finest laser suite.
