THE TREATMENT

What Is Endolift®?

Endolift® is a revolutionary laser technology developed in Italy, using ultra-fine flexible fibres to deliver laser energy directly beneath the skin. Unlike traditional skin tightening treatments that work from the outside, Endolift® targets the deep dermis and subcutaneous tissue from within — delivering precise, controlled contraction and collagen stimulation at exactly the right depth.

As the only certified Endolift® providers in Birmingham and the West Midlands, Dr Naqvi and Dr Zoya offer patients access to this world-class technology in a luxury, doctor-led setting — without the risks, scarring, or lengthy recovery of traditional surgery.

How Endolift® Works

The Endolift® procedure uses a proprietary 1470nm laser wavelength delivered through an ultra-fine flexible optical fibre, inserted via a micro-puncture in the skin — no incisions, no stitching required.

1

Consultation & Mapping

Dr Naqvi conducts a detailed facial assessment, identifying areas of laxity, volume loss, and structural concerns. An individualised treatment map is created for your anatomy.

2

Local Anaesthetic Applied

Topical and localised anaesthetic is administered to ensure complete comfort throughout the procedure. Most patients feel minimal sensation.

3

Laser Fibre Insertion

Ultra-thin optical fibres are inserted sub-dermally through a micro-puncture (smaller than a needle). The laser energy is delivered precisely beneath the skin surface.

4

Tissue Contraction & Collagen Induction

The laser causes immediate tissue contraction (visible tightening) and initiates a prolonged collagen remodelling cascade — with further improvement occurring over 3–6 months.

5

Immediate Recovery

Micro-punctures close within hours. Mild redness and swelling is normal for 24–48 hours. Most patients return to work the same day or the following morning.

Who Is Endolift® For?

Endolift® is ideal for patients who:

  • ✦Are aged 35–65 with early-to-moderate skin laxity in the face, jawline, jowls or neck
  • ✦Want surgical-quality lifting results without surgery, general anaesthetic or weeks of downtime
  • ✦Have good overall skin health and realistic expectations
  • ✦Have been assessed as not yet requiring full surgical facelift intervention
  • ✦Wish to treat the abdomen, inner arm, or knee laxity (body applications available)

Frequently Asked Questions

Most patients report mild pressure or warmth rather than pain. Local anaesthetic is applied prior to the procedure, and most describe the experience as 'comfortable'. Post-procedure, mild tenderness lasting 24–72 hours is common.
Some lifting effect is visible immediately. The full result develops over 3–6 months as collagen remodelling completes. Most patients see the peak of their result at around 3 months.
Clinical data shows results lasting 3–5 years in most patients. The procedure can be repeated after this period. Maintaining a good skincare regimen and skin-boosting treatments (such as Profhilo) can extend longevity.
Most patients experience mild redness and slight swelling for 24–48 hours. Micro-puncture marks (if any) resolve within hours. Most patients return to work the same day or the following morning. No bandaging or dressings required.
A surgical facelift produces more dramatic results but carries significant risks — general anaesthetic, scarring, weeks of downtime, and surgical complications. Endolift® offers comparable lifting for patients with mild-to-moderate laxity, with zero scarring, no general anaesthetic, and immediate recovery. For more advanced laxity, surgery may still be recommended.
Endolift® requires specialist laser certification and physician-level training. Dr Naqvi obtained his Endolift® certification from the Italian Academy and is the only certified provider in the West Midlands. The procedure is only legally performed by medical doctors.
