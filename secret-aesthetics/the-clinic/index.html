OUR STORY

Born from a Vision,
Built on Excellence.

Secret Aesthetics was founded in 2019 by Dr Naqvi and Dr Zoya — two NHS physicians who shared an uncompromising vision: to create Birmingham's most clinically rigorous, aesthetically refined aesthetic clinic. Not a beauty salon with a laser machine. Not a chain franchise. A genuine medical environment, staffed exclusively by physicians, delivering results that match and exceed those of London's Harley Street.

Every treatment is performed personally by our founding doctors. Every consultation is conducted with the thoroughness of a medical appointment. Every outcome is measured against the highest standards of evidence-based aesthetic medicine.

Our exclusive location within Harvey Nichols at The Mailbox reflects our position as Birmingham's most premium aesthetic clinic — and our commitment to providing an exceptional patient experience from the moment you arrive.

OUR PRINCIPLES

What Sets Us Apart

🩺

Doctor-Led Only

Every treatment at Secret Aesthetics is performed personally by Dr Naqvi or Dr Zoya — both MBBS graduates with specialist postgraduate training. We do not employ beauticians, nurses or therapists to administer injectables or laser treatments.

🛡️

CQC Regulated

We are registered with and regulated by the Care Quality Commission — the independent regulator of health and social care in England. This is the mark of a genuine medical facility and is a legal requirement for the procedures we perform.

🔬

Evidence-Based Medicine

We never offer treatments that lack clinical evidence. Our protocols are based on peer-reviewed research, international consensus guidelines, and manufacturer-authorised techniques. We are proud Galderma UK Faculty members.

✦

Birmingham Exclusive

We are the only clinic in Birmingham and the West Midlands certified to offer Endolift® laser lifting. We invested heavily in acquiring this technology and the specialist training to offer it — because our patients deserve access to world-leading treatments.

🏆

Multi Award Winning

Secret Aesthetics has been recognised at 7 national awards ceremonies including the Aesthetics Awards, the MyFaceMyBody Awards, and the Prestige Practitioners Awards — recognising clinical excellence, patient satisfaction, and innovation.

💙

Pro Bono Programme

We run the UK's first doctor-led pro bono aesthetic programme — providing complimentary treatments to patients whose confidence has been impacted by medical conditions, domestic abuse, scarring or significant life events. Beauty, for everyone.
