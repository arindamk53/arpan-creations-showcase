What We Offer

Services Offered by High Five Care

We offer a wide range of services to help manage your health and wellness, from routine screenings to specialized treatments. Our goal is to provide personalized care that supports your well-being.

Primary Care

Comprehensive healthcare for patients 6 years and older, addressing everyday health needs, annual check-ups, and coordination of care with specialists.

Preventative Medicine

Focused on early detection and disease prevention through screenings, immunizations, and personalized health risk assessments.

Acute Disease Management

Timely diagnosis and treatment of sudden illnesses such as infections, minor injuries, and flu-like symptoms to help patients recover quickly.

Chronic Disease Management

Ongoing care and support for conditions like diabetes, hypertension, and asthma, with personalized treatment plans to improve long-term health outcomes.

Men's Care

Health services tailored for men, including testosterone screenings, prostate health, cardiovascular risk assessments, and lifestyle counseling.

Women's Care

Dedicated care for women's health needs, including gynecological screenings, hormone evaluations, and wellness planning through all life stages.

IV + Nutritional Therapy

Custom IV infusions and nutritional support to boost energy, immunity, hydration, and overall well-being, administered in a comfortable setting.

On-Site Testing

Convenient in-house testing including blood work, rapid diagnostics, and screening panels for efficient and accurate results.

Weight Loss Management

Medically supervised weight loss programs tailored to individual needs, including nutrition guidance, metabolic support, and progress tracking.

Sports Physicals

Thorough physical exams for student and adult athletes to ensure they're healthy and ready for participation in sports and physical activities.

Meet Our Provider

Dr. Abdulla H. Abdulla

Family Medicine Physician · Memorial Hermann Health System

Dr. Abdulla H. Abdulla is a board-certified Family Medicine physician with extensive experience practicing in the United States since 2015. He earned his medical degree from Al-Mustansiriyah School of Medicine in Baghdad, Iraq in 2002, completed his residency in Chicago at Swedish Covenant Hospital, and became board-certified in Family Medicine in 2015.

Following his residency, Dr. Abdulla joined the University of Maryland Upper Chesapeake Hospital as a full-time hospitalist. His passion for improving patient well-being and minimizing suffering led him to serve as Medical Director at Endings Hospice Care, where he worked to alleviate pain for patients with terminal illnesses.

After relocating to Houston, Texas, Dr. Abdulla joined Memorial Hermann Health System as a hospitalist at the Cypress campus — treating patients like family, providing follow-up care even after discharge, always going above and beyond in his personal time.

With a dream of establishing his own practice, Dr. Abdulla built High Five Care to create a space where he can not only provide high-quality care but build a team that educates, supports, and grows together. He looks forward to serving the community of Fort Bend County.
